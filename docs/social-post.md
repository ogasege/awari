## Medium (LinkedIn, Facebook)

For Kiroween 2026 (Resurrection) I rebuilt the thing I loved as a kid: the
encyclopedia on a disc that worked with no internet.

Awari ("discovery" in Yoruba) is free, open and offline-first. Read photo-led
articles on 197 countries, tap any word for its meaning, spin a globe, take
quizzes whose hints teach (a nudge, where to look, two answers removed), walk a
trivia castle, and fill a stamp book out of Africa's 54 countries. After one
visit, every feature works in airplane mode, and I wrote a script that proves
it by switching a real browser offline.

I built it spec-first with Kiro: 13 specs in EARS, 87 property-based tests, and
a dated log of what drove each piece of work. Content is all openly licensed
(Simple English Wikipedia, Wikidata, Natural Earth, Open English WordNet,
Wikimedia Commons) and credited on every page.
