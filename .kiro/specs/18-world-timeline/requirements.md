# Requirements: World timeline

## Introduction

The timeline grows from 123 to at least 400 events across every subject
and every continent. (This extends 07-timeline; its rules still hold.)

## Requirements

1. THE pack SHALL hold at least 400 events.
2. EVERY subject (Countries, Electricity, Wildlife, Space, Ancient worlds
   and history, Earth, The human body, Physics and chemistry, Inventions
   and technology, Famous people, Arts, music and sport) SHALL have at
   least 15 events.
3. EACH event MAY name a continent; every one of the seven continents
   (Africa, Asia, Europe, North America, South America, Oceania,
   Antarctica) SHALL have at least three events, and the six inhabited
   continents at least 20.
4. WHERE an event is for a subject pack article or a country, THE event
   SHALL link to that article.
5. WHEN a curated item states the date it expects, and Wikidata gives a
   different day, THEN THE Pipeline SHALL drop the event and report it.
6. THE screen SHALL filter by subject and, together, by continent, with
   toggle buttons that say what is pressed, and SHALL list the count.
7. THE pack SHALL still meet 07-timeline's rules: sentence templates from
   Wikidata fields, day precision, one date, CE, suitable for ages 9-14,
   at least 40 distinct month-and-day pairs (now at least 200).
