# Requirements: Castle wings

## Requirements

1. THE Castle SHALL have one wing for each continent (Africa, Asia,
   Europe, North America, South America, Oceania) and one for each
   subject pack (Electricity, Wildlife, Space, Ancient worlds and history,
   Earth, The human body, Physics and chemistry, Inventions and
   technology, Famous people, Arts, music and sport): 16 wings of twelve
   rooms.
2. A continent wing SHALL draw its questions from the country quizzes of
   that continent; a subject wing from that pack's quizzes.
3. EVERY wing's question pool SHALL hold at least twelve questions, so
   each wing can be completed.
4. THE gate page SHALL group the wings as "Around the world" and
   "Subjects" and show each wing's lit doors; progress SHALL be saved
   per wing on the device.
5. THE existing rules (a right answer lights the next room, a reveal
   keeps it dark, neighbours differ) SHALL still hold.
