# Requirements: Home draws from the whole world

## Introduction

Home keeps its design, but what it shows rotates daily across the whole
world and every subject.

## Requirements

1. THE five subject prints SHALL show five different subjects chosen by
   the date, each with a lead photo from one of that subject's articles,
   and SHALL link to `/subject/:id`.
2. OVER any eleven consecutive days, EVERY subject SHALL appear in the
   prints at least once.
3. THE subject margin notes SHALL be drawn by date from a pool of notes
   built from the articles of the subjects shown that day: a fact note's
   text SHALL be backed by an exact proof sentence of the article it links
   to. THE hero notes SHALL stay fixed, because their drawn arrows point at
   real places on the Earth photograph (changed after the 01-13 review
   pass: rotating hero notes pointed at the wrong places).
4. THE stamp book on Home SHALL show the latest stamps, the count "N of
   197 countries", and a suggestion for the next country chosen by date
   from a continent the student has fewest stamps in.
5. "On this day" SHALL show up to three events for today's month and day
   across subjects; WHEN fewer than three exist, it SHALL fill with the
   next events on the calendar and say so ("Coming up").
6. THE picks SHALL be the same all day, and SHALL not need the network.
